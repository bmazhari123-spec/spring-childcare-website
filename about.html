About Spring Childcare

A licensed neighborhood early learning center built around warm relationships and play-based learning, based in North Vancouver, BC.

Who We Are

Early Learning, Grounded in Care

Spring Childcare is a licensed neighborhood early learning center with certified ECE educators, providing care for infants and toddlers ages 0–3. Based in North Vancouver, BC, we welcome families within approximately a 25km radius — from full-day infant care to our senior toddler program.

Our goal is simple: build a warm, consistent environment where every child feels safe, seen, and excited to learn.
